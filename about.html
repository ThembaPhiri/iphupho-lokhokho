Our Story

Who We Are

Iphupho Lokhokho Shuttles (PTY) Ltd is a proudly South African transport and car rental company based in Durban Central, KwaZulu-Natal. Our name — which means "the dream of the ancestors" — reflects our commitment to building something lasting, meaningful, and community-driven.

We were founded with a simple purpose: to provide Durban residents, tourists, and corporate clients with a reliable, affordable, and professional transport solution they can depend on — day or night, rain or shine.

From our humble beginnings, we've grown to operate a carefully selected fleet of vehicles suited to every occasion — from airport runs to family road trips. We're proud to serve our community and beyond, with services extending across the greater KwaZulu-Natal region.
