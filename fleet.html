Before You Rent

Rental Requirements

To rent a vehicle, please ensure you have the following documents ready.

🪪

Valid Driver's Licence

South African or international driver's licence required. Must be valid and unendorsed.

📇

ID or Passport

South African ID document or valid passport for foreign nationals renting a vehicle.

💳

Payment & Deposit

A refundable deposit is required at time of collection. EFT or cash payment accepted.

📅

Minimum Age: 23

Drivers must be at least 23 years of age and have held their licence for a minimum of 2 years.
