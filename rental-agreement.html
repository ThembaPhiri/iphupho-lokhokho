Car Rental Agreement

The terms that apply to all vehicle rentals with Iphupho Lokhokho Shuttles.

Last Updated: March 2026 | Applies to all vehicle rentals with Iphupho Lokhokho Shuttles (PTY) Ltd

This Rental Agreement ("Agreement") sets out the terms and conditions governing all vehicle rentals provided by Iphupho Lokhokho Shuttles (PTY) Ltd ("the Company"). By collecting and using a rental vehicle, the Renter agrees to be bound by this Agreement.

1. Eligibility to Rent

  • The Renter must be at least 23 years of age.
  • The Renter must hold a valid, unendorsed South African driver's licence or valid international driver's licence.
  • The Renter must have held a driver's licence for a minimum of 2 (two) years.
  • The Renter must present a valid South African ID document or passport (for foreign nationals).
  • Only the named Renter and approved additional drivers listed in this agreement are authorised to drive the vehicle.

2. Vehicle Condition at Collection

The Renter is responsible for inspecting the vehicle at collection and noting any pre-existing damage on the Vehicle Inspection Form provided. Failure to report damage at collection may result in the Renter being held liable for such damage upon return.

All vehicles are provided clean, roadworthy, and with a full tank of fuel unless otherwise agreed.

3. Security Deposit

A refundable security deposit is required at the time of vehicle collection. The deposit amount depends on the vehicle selected and will be communicated at the time of booking confirmation. The deposit is returned within 3–5 business days of vehicle return, subject to inspection and confirmation that the vehicle has been returned in good condition.

4. Renter's Responsibilities

The Renter agrees to:

  • Return the vehicle on the agreed date, time, and location in the same condition as collected.
  • Return the vehicle with a full tank of fuel (or as agreed).
  • Not allow any unauthorised person to drive the vehicle.
  • Not drive the vehicle under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
  • Not use the vehicle for any illegal purpose or off-road driving (unless pre-approved).
  • Not transport more passengers than the vehicle's stated capacity.
  • Not smoke in the vehicle.
  • Notify the Company immediately in the event of an accident, breakdown, or theft.
  • Comply with all South African road laws and speed limits.

5. Insurance

All rental vehicles are covered by the Company's motor vehicle insurance. However, in the event of an accident where the Renter is found to be at fault, the Renter will be liable for an excess amount as specified in the booking confirmation.

The Company's insurance does not cover:

  • Damage caused by driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs
  • Damage caused by reckless or negligent driving
  • Damage to tyres, rims, or the undercarriage from off-road use
  • Loss of personal belongings from the vehicle
  • Damage caused by an unauthorised driver

6. Accidents and Breakdowns

In the event of an accident: Do not move the vehicle if there are injuries. Call emergency services (10111 police / 10177 ambulance) if required. Contact the Company immediately at +27 (0) 00 000 0000. Obtain details of all parties involved. Do not admit liability.

In the event of a breakdown: Contact the Company immediately. Do not attempt repairs yourself unless authorised. The Company will arrange roadside assistance or a replacement vehicle where possible.

7. Traffic Fines

The Renter is fully responsible for any traffic fines, toll fees, or penalties incurred during the rental period. If the Company receives a fine relating to the rental period, the full fine amount plus an administration fee will be charged to the Renter.

8. Late Returns

Vehicles must be returned by the agreed date and time. Late returns without prior notification will be charged at the applicable daily rate for each additional day or part thereof.

9. Early Returns

Early returns are permitted, but no refund will be provided for unused rental days unless otherwise agreed at the time of booking.

10. Dispute Resolution

Any disputes arising from this Agreement will first be addressed through direct negotiation between the parties. If unresolved, disputes will be referred to mediation before any legal proceedings are initiated. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of South Africa.
