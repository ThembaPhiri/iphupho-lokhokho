Shuttle Services

Shuttle FAQs

Do you offer airport pickup services?

+
Yes! Airport transfers to and from King Shaka International Airport are one of our most popular services. We monitor your flight arrival so that your driver is there when you land, even if your flight is delayed. Just provide your flight number at the time of booking.

How far in advance should I book a shuttle?

+
We recommend booking at least 24 hours in advance to guarantee availability. For airport transfers during peak periods (early mornings, weekends, or public holidays), we suggest booking 48 hours ahead. For urgent same-day bookings, WhatsApp or call us directly and we'll do our best to accommodate you.

What areas do you cover?

+
We are based in Durban Central and cover the entire greater Durban metropolitan area, including Umhlanga, Ballito, Pinetown, Chatsworth, Tongaat, Umlazi, and surrounding suburbs. We also provide long-distance trips to Pietermaritzburg, the South Coast, and other KwaZulu-Natal destinations. Contact us for a quote on any route.

Can I book a shuttle for a large group?

+
Yes. Our Toyota Rumion seats up to 7 passengers and is ideal for group travel. For larger groups requiring multiple vehicles, please contact us directly to discuss your requirements and we'll put together a tailored quote.

Are your drivers professional and licensed?

+
Absolutely. All our drivers are professionally licensed, have undergone background checks, and are trained to provide a safe, courteous, and professional service. They are familiar with Durban's roads and will always choose the best route for your trip.
Car Rental

Rental FAQs

What documents are required to rent a car?

+
To rent a vehicle, you will need: (1) A valid, unendorsed South African driver's licence or valid international driver's licence, (2) A South African ID document or valid passport for foreign nationals, and (3) Payment for the rental plus a refundable security deposit. The minimum rental age is 23 years and you must have held your licence for at least 2 years.

Is a deposit required for car rentals?

+
Yes. A refundable security deposit is required at the time of vehicle collection. The deposit amount varies depending on the vehicle selected. This deposit is fully refunded upon return of the vehicle in good condition, subject to a vehicle inspection.

Can I drive the rental car outside of Durban or KwaZulu-Natal?

+
Travel within KwaZulu-Natal is generally permitted. For travel outside the province, please inform us at the time of booking so we can make the necessary arrangements and update the rental agreement. Additional charges may apply for extended distances.

Do you offer long-term car rental?

+
Yes, we offer flexible long-term rental options for weekly, monthly, and extended periods. Long-term rentals often come with discounted rates. Contact us directly to discuss your specific needs and we'll put together a suitable package for you.

What is included in the rental price?

+
The rental price includes the vehicle, basic insurance, and unlimited kilometres (within agreed travel zones). Fuel is the responsibility of the renter — you will receive the vehicle with a full tank and are expected to return it full. Additional charges may apply for optional extras such as chauffeur service or out-of-area travel.
Payments & Policies

Payments & Policies

What payment methods do you accept?

+
We accept cash, EFT (electronic bank transfer), and online card payments via PayFast and PayGate. Payment can be made in advance to confirm your booking or upon collection/completion of service. For corporate clients, monthly invoicing is available upon account setup.

What is the cancellation policy?

+
Cancellations made more than 24 hours before the scheduled trip or rental are free of charge. Cancellations within 24 hours of the booking may incur a cancellation fee. No-shows will be charged the full trip fee. We understand emergencies happen — please contact us as soon as possible if your plans change and we'll do our best to assist.

What happens if a vehicle breaks down during a rental?

+
In the unlikely event of a mechanical breakdown, please contact us immediately. We will arrange roadside assistance or a replacement vehicle as quickly as possible. Our vehicles are regularly serviced and maintained to minimise the risk of breakdowns.

Are there any age restrictions for renting a vehicle?

+
The minimum age to rent a vehicle is 23 years. You must also have held a valid driver's licence for at least 2 years. There is no maximum age restriction, provided you hold a valid driver's licence.
